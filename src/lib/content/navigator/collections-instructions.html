Material

Material types organise related project material without imposing a fixed writing structure. Examples include Characters, Chapters, Scenes, Locations, Research, Themes, or any category useful to this project.

  • Select a Material type name to edit its own content.
  • Use the disclosure arrow to show or hide its items.
  • Use Manage Material to create a custom Material type or choose editable suggestions from a Material set.
  • Use the move handle to reorder a Material type and its action menu to change its name, icon, or numbering.
  • Deleting a Material type preserves its items under Archived/Unowned.
