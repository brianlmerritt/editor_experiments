Collections

Collections organise related material without imposing a fixed writing structure. Examples include Characters, Chapters, Scenes, Locations, Research, Themes, or any category useful to this project.

  • Select a Collection name to edit its own content.
  • Use the disclosure arrow to show or hide its items.
  • Use Manage Collections to create a custom Collection or choose editable suggestions from a Collection set.
  • Use the move handle to reorder a Collection and its action menu to change its name, icon, or numbering.
  • Deleting a Collection preserves its items under Archived/Unowned.
