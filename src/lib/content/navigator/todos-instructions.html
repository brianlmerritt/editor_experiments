Todos

Todos are writing work, not disposable notification cards. Each Todo has its own editable document for plans, evidence, alternatives, and notes.

  • Select a Todo title to open its content.
  • Use only the checkbox to mark it done or open.
  • A Todo may relate to the Spine, one or more Collections or documents, or the work as a whole.
  • More detailed task hierarchy and multi-target management will build on the same durable Todo identity.
